NESTCHAT / LOCAL AI

Privacy, in plain language.

Privacy Policy · Effective September 9, 2026
Himanshu Rathi · thehimanshurathi@gmail.com

NestChat performs AI text generation on your Android device. There is no developer-operated inference server, NestChat account system, advertising, subscription, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK. Optional AI-content reports use Google Forms inside the app.

Local data
Chats, settings, downloaded models, and memory.md are stored in the app's private Android storage. Memory is saved only when you explicitly edit it or use a Remember action; the newest 24 lines are retained within a 2,400-character total limit. Memory is not shared with local API clients. Android backup is disabled for app data. Data is protected by Android's app sandbox and device security; the app does not promise protection from an unlocked, compromised, or rooted device.

Model downloads
When you tap Download or send the first message to an undownloaded model, the model is fetched over HTTPS from Hugging Face and its download hosts. Those services can receive your IP address, requested file, connection information, and download timing under their own policies. No chat text or memory is included in download requests. Downloads may use mobile data. There are no automatic catalog refreshes or remote configuration requests.

Optional local API
This feature is off by default and after process restart. When explicitly enabled, authorized clients on a private Wi-Fi network or personal hotspot can send prompts and receive generated responses. A generated bearer token is required. The server uses unencrypted HTTP; only enable it on a trusted private network. API prompts are processed in memory, are not saved to chat history, and do not receive personal memory. Network peers can potentially observe HTTP traffic. Disable the API or regenerate its token to revoke access.

Google Play reviews
NestChat may show a rating invitation on app launches 3, 14, 25, and every 11 launches afterward. Returning from the background does not count as a new launch. Not now or dismissing the invitation keeps this schedule. Tapping Rate now, or the manual Review on Google Play button in Settings, saves a permanent local preference to stop automatic rating reminders before opening the Google Play listing. Reminders stay off even if you do not submit a rating, Play is unavailable, or you restart or update the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes this preference. You can also disable reminders in Settings before choosing to rate. NestChat does not receive whether you rated or submitted a review. Google processes information you submit to Google Play under its own privacy practices. No chat text, memory, or local rating-reminder history is sent with the listing link.

Files, clipboard, and exports
The system file picker grants access only to text files you choose. Selected text can become part of a local conversation. Copy and export actions place selected content on the clipboard or pass it to another app you choose. That app's privacy practices then apply. Keyboard software and Android system services have their own privacy controls.

Optional AI-content reports
Reporting first shows a local preview. Excerpt-sharing checkboxes are off by default. You may edit and include up to 1,200 characters of a response and 600 characters of the preceding user message, or describe the issue without excerpts. Memory and the rest of your conversation are not automatically included. Opening the form connects to Google and prefills app version, model and report source when known. Included excerpts are sent to Google in the prefill URL when the form opens, before you press Submit.

Google may receive your IP address, browser/device information, cookies and form interactions under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . No Google sign-in is required by the configured form. The embedded browser disables third-party cookies and local file access. Its cache and cookies are cleared when you close the reporting screen normally; Android app storage can also be cleared.

Submitted reports are stored in Himanshu Rathi's Google Forms account and may be linked to a private Google Sheet. The developer can receive response notifications by email. The form asks about the issue, relevant text, model, source, thinking mode and app version; contact email is optional. Reports are used to investigate issues and improve content safeguards and retained while needed for those purposes. Request deletion at thehimanshurathi@gmail.com with sufficient information to identify the report, such as its date and description. Deleting chats or uninstalling the app does not remove reports already submitted. Google and email providers may retain service logs under their own policies.

Local hiding
Hide response on this phone hides a response and excludes it from future chat context locally. This action does not send a report. Local hidden-response markers are deleted with the corresponding chat.

Permissions
Internet enables requested model downloads, optional Google Forms reporting and the optional local API. Opening Google Play or other external links may also use a network connection in the receiving app. Network state supports connectivity information. Foreground service and wake lock permissions support user-started downloads, local generation, and the local API. Optional notifications show progress and a stop action. No contacts, location, microphone, camera, or broad storage permissions are requested.

Retention and deletion
Chats stay until you delete them. Reset memory in Settings; remove models from the library; delete all chats from Settings. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove all local app data. Copies you exported and third-party download logs are outside the app's control. No data is sold.

Children and accuracy
The app is not specifically directed at children. Models can produce inaccurate or inappropriate responses. Check important information. Guardians should supervise children's use of general-purpose AI.

Changes and contact
Future versions may update this policy. The effective date will be updated when practices change. For privacy questions contact Himanshu Rathi at thehimanshurathi@gmail.com. If you email support, your email provider and the developer receive the information you choose to send.
